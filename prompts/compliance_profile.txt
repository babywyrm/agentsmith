GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  Priority: Art. 5 (data minimization), Art. 25 (privacy by design), Art. 32 (security of processing), Art. 17 (right to erasure)
  Look for: PII stored without encryption, no consent mechanism, missing data deletion capability, excessive data collection, logging PII, no data retention limits
  Severity: CRITICAL if PII is unencrypted or no deletion path exists

HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)
  Priority: PHI protection, access controls, audit trails, minimum necessary rule
  Look for: Health data in plaintext, missing access logging, no role-based access to PHI, PHI in logs/errors
  Severity: CRITICAL if PHI is exposed without access controls

PCI-DSS v4.0
  Priority: Req 3 (protect stored data), Req 4 (encrypt transmission), Req 6 (secure development), Req 8 (access controls)
  Look for: Card data in plaintext, PAN without masking, missing TLS, hardcoded credentials, debug endpoints
  Severity: CRITICAL if card data is stored unencrypted

CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  Priority: Consumer rights (access, deletion, opt-out), data disclosure, sale of data
  Look for: No opt-out mechanism, data shared without disclosure, missing data access endpoint
  Severity: HIGH if no consumer data rights are implemented

SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley)
  Priority: Financial data integrity, audit trails, access controls, change management
  Look for: Financial data modifications without audit trail, missing access controls on financial operations
  Severity: HIGH if financial operations lack audit logging
